Why Melitrix

Built for the long horizon

Melitrix is privately held and not raising capital. That lets us choose programs and partners on merit, and stay with them.

Integrated, not scattered

Development, regulatory, manufacturing, and commercial capability sit under one roof, so a program does not lose momentum handing off between vendors.

Global from day one

Our manufacturing and R&D partners are established operators in Europe and India, with distribution reach into EMEA and emerging markets.

Regulatory literacy

We work fluently across ANDA, 505(b)(2), EU-GMP, and consumer health frameworks, and we plan submissions before we start formulation.

Patient outcome first

Every program has to show a credible path to a better outcome or better access. Commercial opportunity alone is not a reason to advance one.
