How we hire

Range over specialization

We value operators who have crossed disciplines, science to commercial, regulation to capital, manufacturing to strategy. Depth matters. So does the willingness to learn the next adjacent domain.

Demonstrated outcomes

Programs delivered, products launched, dossiers filed, audits passed. We weight track record and judgment higher than credentials.

Trust as a baseline

We invest in long, candid relationships with the people we work with. Quality of character is non-negotiable.

Patient orientation

Every role at Melitrix exists in service of patient outcomes. We expect new team members to share that orientation from day one.
